About The Band

Based out of Kitsap County, Morgan Smith & Co. plays bluegrass and acoustic roots music built for taprooms, outdoor stages, and everything in between.

Rooted in traditional picking and grown in the evergreen forests of Washington, the lineup brings together fast-picking acoustic instrumentation, warm vocal harmonies, and a deep appreciation for the region's music community.

From local spots in Poulsbo and Silverdale to venues across Western Washington and the PNW, they bring an honest, high-energy performance everywhere they set up.
