About The Band

Morgan Smith & Co. is a premier string band delivering authentic Evergreen Bluegrass and Americana across the Pacific Northwest. Rooted in traditional mountain music heritage but driven by modern, high-energy acoustic picking, the group brings a fresh, natural flavor to stages throughout Washington state.

From powerful lead vocals and classic driving banjo lines to sophisticated mandolin and upright bass grooves, their performances bridge the gap between old-time storytelling and contemporary folk energy. Whether filling festival fields or picking acoustic sets in local craft breweries and wineries, Morgan Smith & Co. captures the rugged spirit of the timberlands they call home.
